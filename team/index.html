Team Jengara

The people building Jengara.

Jengara B.V. is an AI software company registered in the Netherlands. This page introduces the founder who leads it and explains how the company is organised.

Leadership

Frank Ribeiro

Founder and CEO, Jengara B.V.

Frank founded Jengara in August 2026 to build AI products for enterprise teams. Jengara B.V. is registered in Amstelveen, the Netherlands (KvK 42186593).

He brings fifteen years in network reliability and digital infrastructure. His background includes Netflix, where he works as a Senior Network Reliability Engineer, AWS in Dublin, where he was a Network Development Engineer, and earlier telecoms work in Ghana and Europe. He is CCIE certified. At Jengara he leads product and engineering across Jengara Labs, home to Ribyos, Tesseryn and Prompt Presence.

Our structure

How Jengara is organised

Jengara works through two arms, both led by the founder.

Jengara Labs builds the products: Ribyos for enterprise knowledge and agent orchestration, Tesseryn for network exposure and change assurance, and Prompt Presence for AI visibility.

Jengara Research publishes product and engineering notes on what the team learns by building, at jengara.ai/insights.
