Access is the new frontier model tier

By Frank Ribeiro, Jengara · 17 November 2026 · Product and engineering notes

Access is now a model tier. In November 2026 the strongest models from all three leading laboratories arrived with an eligibility condition attached, so the capability an enterprise can deploy is set as much by vetting and retention terms as by benchmark scores or list price.

Research approach

Jengara reviewed the Laurenceo Intelligence frontier models briefing updated on 16 November 2026, Anthropic guidance on data retention for Covered Models, the Tautoru briefing of 12 November 2026, and reporting on GPT 6 Cyber, Gemini 4 Carbon and Mistral Large 4. Every figure traces to one of those sources.

The frontier now splits by eligibility

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 in November 2026. The two share the same underlying model. Fable carries added safeguards and is available broadly. Mythos is offered only to organisations vetted through the Cyber Verification Program and the Life Sciences Verification Program.

OpenAI made the same split on 5 November 2026. GPT 6 Cyber is a specialist version of GPT 6 Astra built for authorised security work. OpenAI rates it Critical for cyber capability under its Preparedness Framework, up from High for the prior generation, and offers it only to approved Daybreak Red customers. Google lists a separate cyber permissive variant of Gemini 4 Carbon beside the standard model, reached through its Fairwind Program under a distinct model identifier.

Capability you can buy is not capability you can use

Price is no longer the binding term even where a model is public. Anthropic cut Fable 5.2 to 8 dollars per million input tokens and 40 dollars per million output tokens, from 10 and 50 dollars. Yet Fable sits on the Covered Models list. Anthropic states that prompts sent to Covered Models and outputs from them are retained for 30 days to support safety work on every platform where the models are offered, and that zero data retention is generally unavailable for them.

The tier below carries different terms. Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 can be used with zero data retention. A legal, health or finance workload that depends on zero retention may therefore have to stay on a weaker model, not by choice of budget but by rule of access. A comparison that lists only scores and prices misses the term that decides the deployment.

The gap behind the gate is real

On version 5 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, read on 12 November 2026, Claude Fable 5.2 scores 65 and Gemini 4 Carbon scores 60. The strongest open weight model, Moonshot Kimi K3.1 Pro, scores 46, a gap of 19 points behind the best closed model.

The security results explain why laboratories gate first. OpenAI says GPT 6 Cyber helped find 81 previously unknown vulnerabilities in Firefox that Mozilla classed as high severity, and produced working exploits for 948 Linux kernel vulnerabilities. Google reports that Gemini 4 Carbon scored 54.8 percent on ExploitGym without guardrails. A capability that compresses the time between finding and exploiting a flaw will not be sold on a public checkout page.

Eligibility is a procurement workstream

Treat eligibility as a project with a lead time. Daybreak Red applicants must describe their intended defensive workflows. Coverage of the programme describes identity verification, legal attestations, a hardware security key for each user, and API access restricted to approved networks by default. Google routes Fairwind applications through the customer account team, and access to one cyber variant does not carry over to the next.

Assign an owner before the workload depends on the model. Procurement should record eligibility beside price in the vendor file, with the date granted and the conditions that could see it withdrawn. A roadmap that assumes gated access from day one carries an unpriced schedule risk.

Build the fallback before you need it

Every gated deployment needs a tested fallback that runs without the gate, whether a public tier, a second laboratory, or open weights on infrastructure the enterprise controls. Mistral released the weights of Large 4, a trillion parameter model, on 29 October 2026, giving regulated buyers a self hostable option at frontier scale. It scores 26 on the Artificial Analysis index, well below the closed leaders, so the trade is explicit. Control rises as capability falls.

Test the fallback on the real workload. Route production tasks to it each month and measure quality and cost per completed task. If the fallback has never carried live work, it is a hope rather than a plan, and the gated model has quietly become a single point of failure.

Jengara decision rule

Do not commit a production workload to a gated model until three things are proven in writing. First, the organisation holds eligibility for the exact variant the workload will call. Second, the retention terms of that tier pass the data review for the workload. Third, a fallback model has carried the same workload at an accepted quality level. If any one is missing, design for the strongest model the organisation can use today and treat the gated tier as upside.

Executive takeaway

Model selection has become an access decision. Boards should ask which model the organisation is allowed to use, on what data terms, and what runs when that permission is delayed or withdrawn.
