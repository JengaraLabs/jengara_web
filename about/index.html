About Jengara

Building AI software for real-world needs.

Jengara BV is a Netherlands-incorporated AI software company. Through Jengara Labs, we design and develop proprietary platforms for enterprise AI orchestration, infrastructure risk intelligence, and generative AI visibility.

Our model

Product development leads our work.

Our mission is to turn organisational knowledge into accountable AI-driven action. Ribyos brings together Studio for building agents, Enterprise for deployment and governance, and Marketplace for reusable extensions.

Our work focuses on product engineering, applied AI research, dependable operations and useful customer outcomes. Forward deployed engineering supports enterprise design partners with product integration, implementation and deployment feedback. This capability is dedicated to Jengara Labs products, not standalone consulting.

Our structure

Product engineering is our focus.

Jengara Labs develops RibyOS, Tesseryn and PromptPresence, each addressing a distinct enterprise challenge.

RibyOS and Tesseryn target design-partner onboarding in January 2027. PromptPresence targets its initial launch in October 2026.
